SIPPULSE INTERNAL

Meet Capture

Live, speaker-labelled Google Meet transcripts with AI notes. The final transcript and meeting report are saved to SipPulse CRM and emailed to the capturing collaborator.

Before you enable capture

  • Google caption text is captured automatically as a fallback.
  • Live transcription starts only when you click it. It streams the Meet tab audio and your microphone to the transcription provider your organization configured (Deepgram or SipPulse AI), which returns the transcript; audio is not stored by the extension.
  • The transcript is sent to SipPulse AI during and after the call to produce notes: summary, topics, action items, decisions, and open questions.
  • Each transcribed line is sent to TypeSafe (Jev model) to classify its intent, sentiment, and whether it is an action item.
  • The final vCon, including the meeting report, is stored in SipPulse CRM and emailed to your company email.

Use capture only when meeting participants have been informed and the recording is permitted by company policy and applicable law.
