Meet Capture

Save a diarized meeting transcript to SipPulse CRM and send a copy to the capturing collaborator's company email.

Before you enable capture

  • Google caption text is captured automatically as a fallback.
  • High-quality mode records the Meet tab and your microphone only after you start it.
  • Audio is sent to SipPulse for transcription with pulse-telephony, speaker diarization, and automatic language detection.
  • The final vCon is stored in SipPulse CRM and the diarized transcript is emailed to your company email.

Use capture only when meeting participants have been informed and the recording is permitted by company policy and applicable law.
