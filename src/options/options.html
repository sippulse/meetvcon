SIPPULSE INTERNAL

Meet Capture

Live, speaker-labelled Google Meet transcripts with AI notes. The final transcript and meeting report are saved to your organization's vCon store and emailed to the capturing collaborator. The live view is Chrome's side panel, opened from the extension button.

Before you enable capture

  • Google caption text is captured as a fallback when you have captions on. The extension turns captions on by itself only where your administrator made them the transcript source (see Settings).
  • Live transcription starts only when you click it. It streams the Meet tab audio and your microphone to SipPulse AI, which returns the transcript; audio is not stored by the extension. On the Google captions source no audio leaves this tab at all.
  • The transcript is sent to SipPulse AI during and after the call to produce notes: summary, topics, action items, decisions, and open questions.
  • Each transcribed line is sent to TypeSafe (Jev model) to classify its intent, sentiment, and whether it is an action item.
  • The final vCon, including the meeting report, is stored in your organization's vCon store and emailed to your company email.

Use capture only when meeting participants have been informed and the recording is permitted by company policy and applicable law.
